# MicroCOM 2026 station panel (M2026) and producer panel (M2026P)

The 2026 panel is the successor of the SVG Ward-Beck M7880 keypanel. It keeps the hardware look (brushed 19"
faceplate, gooseneck mic with TALK buttons, black paddle keys, red LED legends, 4x4 keypad, knobs, WBS badge)
and turns parts of it into live screens. Code: `src/m2026/`, tests: `test/m2026/`.

| Model | Size | Keys | Extra |
|---|---|---|---|
| `M2026` | 4RU | 32 (2 rows of 16) | screen in the vent grille, map in the speaker grille, LED ticker |
| `M2026P` | 5RU | 32 | plus a talent strip (8 IFB/Mic positions) and a production-wide screen |

Keys are filled like the M7832: MCOM-programmed keys first, then matrix (extended) keys, then blanks.

## Quick look

1. Open the matrix (`http://127.0.0.1:8048/`), select a station, e.g. **PROD01 PNL02** (the producer).
2. In the keypanel header, change the model picker from `M7832 — 32 keys` to **`M2026P — MicroCOM 2026 producer`**
   (or `M2026 — MicroCOM 2026 station`). The panel redraws at once.
3. Press **Open panel ↗** for the full-size page (`/panel/PROD01/PNL02`); token links (`?k=…`) show it too.
4. Switch back by choosing `M7832` again. The demo data is unchanged; the model is saved in the engine state.

The same can be done from a script: `{t:'panelmodel', gid, model:'M2026P'}` (operator connection).

## What is on the panel

### Vent grille → "Recently said" screen
* Everything said **to this station** (and by it) in the last 10 minutes, from the engine's `history`
  (requested on open and after every reconnect) plus live `said` messages. Captions arrive a few seconds
  later (local speech-to-text) and replace "Captioning…".
* Focused item: emoji, name, role, mnemonic, how long ago, duration, caption. **▶** replays the WAV clip
  (`/api/clip/<id>.wav`, with the page's `?k=` token) and highlights the word being spoken.
* Right column: the 5 latest items (click to focus). Bottom: a **10-minute scrubber** with one bar per segment
  (overlapping talkers on separate lanes). Drag to scrub back ("if it was the director and it's too late, it
  still shows up"); click a bar to play it; the right edge = LIVE (follow the newest).
* **Unread / missed**: unread items are amber (dot over the bar, "NEW"); items that arrived while this
  page's intercom audio was off are **MISSED** (red). Focusing an item for 1.5 s or replaying it marks it
  read; **ACK** (badge or keypad) marks all read. Read state is kept per station in `localStorage`.
* On the producer panel the screen shows **everything said across the production** ("who → to whom").

### Keys → people
Each key has a 3-line LED window: status LED, the target's emoji and the MCOM mnemonic; the person's name
(shortened: "Rosa Moreau" → "Rosa M." → "Rosa" → initials, else it scrolls); the role (abbreviated or
scrolling). Phantom keys show their function (Group, Conf, Call…). Without metadata the mnemonic is used.
A key whose target is **talking to you** glows amber ("calling"). Picking a person on the map flashes the keys
that reach them.

### Speaker grille → map ("here, there, everywhere")
* **HERE** – this room: rows and seats from `location.row/seat/x/y`; your row is highlighted ("people in this
  row"), front of the room at the top.
* **THERE** – the production's floor plan: its rooms (control room, studio…) packed side by side, everyone
  plotted in place.
* **EVERYWHERE** – a globe (orthographic projection, drawn from an embedded ~5 KB coastline) or a flat world
  map, with every site from `facMeta` and the production's remote positions.
* **MAP** cycles globe → world → floor plan → room. Live state relative to this station: red pulse = talking
  to you, amber = you are talking to them, green ring = you hear them; talk arcs (great circles) from your
  site to remote sites on the globe/world. Learned same-room neighbours (proximity) get a violet ring and a
  dashed line. The info strip shows the selection and where it is relative to you: "1.6 m at 3 o'clock" in
  the same room, "3,020 km E (86°)" elsewhere.

### Production selector (on top of the map)
`◀ PROD29 · Travel Show · Calgary ▶` switches which production the map (and the producer screen) shows;
click the name to come home. Amber = viewing another production.

### 4x4 keypad → navigation
`HERE ▲ THERE ZM+ / ◀ OK ▶ ZM− / WORLD ▼ ME MAP / ◀PRD ACK PRD▶ PLAY`. Arrows move the selection to the nearest
station/site in that direction (the globe rotates to it; with nothing that way the globe/world pans); **OK**
drills in (site → its floor, station → its room, person → flash their keys); ZM−at the minimum zooms out a
level. With the panel focused the keyboard works too: arrows, Enter, `+`/`-`, `h`/`t`/`w` (levels), `m` (map),
`e` (me), PgUp/PgDn (step through what was said), `p` (play), `k` (ack), `[` `]` (production). Number keys still
operate keys 1–10 and A–D as before. Mouse: click markers, wheel = zoom.

### Identity, ticker
Top left: your emoji, name, role, room/row/seat. Click it to set your **name and emoji** (`{t:'meta'}`; a panel
link may only change those two). The LED ticker under the keys reads "▶ TALKING TO … · ◀ HEARING …".

### Producer extras (M2026P)
Eight talent tiles (the production's IFB/Mic ports): name/emoji, 10-segment VU from `{t:'levels'}`, MIC / CGH
(cough) / IFB (interrupted) / PGM lamps, and a **TALK** paddle bound to this panel's key for that IFB (click =
latch, hold = momentary; "—" when the panel has no key for it). **ALL TALENT** operates every IFB key at once.
In the demo the producer's 32 keys hold its 16 MCOM keys plus 16 director keys, so only IF02 has a key.

### Proximity ("knows what is near")
Runs on the panel page whenever intercom audio is joined (any panel model). When the engine announces a blip
for this station, the page plays a 250 ms, −26 dBFS tone at 18.75 kHz (centre of the 18–19.5 kHz band) after
`in` seconds. Every other joined page measures that band on a **second microphone capture** (echo
cancellation, noise suppression and AGC off, device rate, AudioWorklet tap, 1024-sample blocks) with a
Hann-windowed Goertzel over each in-band bin: block power = the 18.75 kHz peak minus the band median (blocks
whose strongest tone is elsewhere count as 0). SNR = strongest block in the blip window vs. the median of the
noise just before (floored at −90 dBFS), reported as `{t:'heard', snr}`. Status is shown under the audio card
("Proximity: listening at 48000 Hz · blips played 1, measured 1 (last 0 dB …) · near: …").

## Real vs simulated

| Real (engine data) | Simulated / local |
|---|---|
| Names, roles, emoji, rooms/rows/seats, site lat/lon (demo data from the engine) | Floor-plan geometry: rooms are packed side by side, not a real building drawing |
| Recently said: real talk segments, WAV clips and local captions — only when real mics stream (join audio) | "Missed" = arrived while this page's audio was off (page-local) |
| Routes, talk/listen state, levels, talent mic/cough | The globe's coastline is a hand-traced low-res outline |
| Proximity reports and learned `near` pairs | In headless/fake-mic tests nothing is acoustically coupled, so real reports are ≈ 0 dB |

Proximity is acoustic and needs real speakers/mics within earshot; devices running at < 40 kHz cannot carry
the band (shown as "unsupported"). A person talking keeps a station "busy", so the engine waits for idle
moments before blipping.

## Known limits (engine side)
* Panel links (`?k=`) only receive their own `levels`, routes and talent state, so on a LAN producer link the
  talent VU/lamps of other positions and other stations' live states stay dark; full on the operator page.
* A panel link's `history` and `said` cover only its own station, so the producer's production-wide screen is
  complete only for the operator (it requests history for every panel/IFB/remote of the production).
* `{t:'near'}` (request) is operator-only; panel links get `near` broadcasts (their own pairs) as they happen.
