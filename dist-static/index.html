MicroCOM Simulator

The Ward-Beck Systems MicroCOM II intercom matrix (1988–93) brought back to life: the original assignment software, the frame it programmed and the panels it drove. Everything runs in your browser. Open several tabs and they share one matrix, so panels in different tabs can talk to each other.

MicroCOM II emulator

The original DOS program, MCOM.EXE v3.20, unmodified, on an emulated 8086 PC. Bring your own copy of MCOM.EXE. Upload to a simulated frame or to the matrix below.

emu.html

Matrix simulator

A 2048-port matrix: 32 productions of panels, IFB and talent positions, remotes and utilities. Live crosspoints, routing and audio.

matrix.html

Panels

M7880 key panels, talent turrets and the new MicroCOM 2026 station and producer panels. Use your microphone and talk between tabs.

MicroCOM III

The assignment software reimagined in the MicroCOM II look, configuring the large virtual matrix.
